 Track 1 — AI Governance, Cost & Value

## Use Case Anchor: KP Vitals Agentic AI Pipeline

This track examines how an AI-enabled healthcare workflow can be governed from initial design through operational scaling.

The analysis focuses on five connected questions:

1. **Trust & Governance** — What controls are required for responsible AI use?
2. **Risk** — Where can AI decisions create operational, clinical, privacy, or compliance risk?
3. **Human Oversight** — Which decisions can be automated and which require human review?
4. **Cost Governance** — How should agent runs, model usage, retries, and AI operating costs be controlled?
5. **Value Measurement** — How should KPI, ROI, reliability, and business outcomes be measured?

## Frameworks Applied

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF)
- NIST AI 600-1
- ISO/IEC 42001
- EU AI Act risk-tier concepts
- OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications
- Human-in-the-Loop control principles
- FinOps concepts for AI
- KPI / ROI measurement

## Portfolio Objective

The purpose of this track is to demonstrate the ability to translate AI governance frameworks into practical workflow-level controls and business decisions rather than treating governance as a standalone compliance exercise.
