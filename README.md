# AI Governance & Transformation Portfolio

## Purpose

This portfolio demonstrates a practical approach to AI governance, risk management, change management, cost governance, KPI/ROI, and AI transformation.

The work is built around realistic healthcare use cases and focuses on how organizations can move from traditional workflows toward responsible, governed AI and agentic AI adoption.

## Portfolio Use Cases

### KP Vitals — Agentic AI Pipeline
**Primary Track 1 anchor**

Focus areas:
- AI governance and trust
- Human-in-the-loop decision gates
- Risk and control design
- Cost and run-budget governance
- KPI and ROI measurement
- Responsible scaling of agentic AI

### Sovera HIM → Agentic AI Transition
**Primary Track 2 anchor**

Focus areas:
- Change management
- Stakeholder adoption
- Risk assessment
- Legacy-system transformation
- Workforce impact
- Trust calibration

### Hospital-at-Home
**Higher-stakes risk and adoption scenario**

Focus areas:
- Patient-safety risk
- FMEA
- Human oversight
- AI adoption

## Approach

The objective is not simply to demonstrate knowledge of AI frameworks.

The objective is to show how an AI transformation professional can translate frameworks into practical decisions involving:

**Governance → Risk → Human Oversight → Change → Cost → KPI/ROI → Adoption → Scale**
